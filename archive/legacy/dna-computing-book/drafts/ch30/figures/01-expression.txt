Eukaryotic expression mechanism. A paired DNA locus with labeled antiparallel ends is contacted by a diffusible transcription factor and RNA polymerase. A nascent RNA leaves the polymerase, then processing/export precedes cytoplasmic ribosomal translation. Solid strokes represent backbones or chain continuity; the dashed TF arrow denotes association, not a covalent bond. DNA is not translated directly; RNA/protein are distinct carriers. Schematic geometry is not molecular scale.
