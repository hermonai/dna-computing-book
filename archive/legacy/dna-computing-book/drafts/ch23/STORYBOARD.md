# DNAD-23 mechanism storyboard

1. Conditional design–assembly–observation–classification channel. Physical faults
   and measurement faults occupy different transitions.
2. Generated IID and beta-mixture clean-object probability versus site count;
   equal marginal error does not imply equal joint yield.
3. Five-bit majority decode with two flips, contrasted with common-mode failure.
4. A logical tile expanded into a cooperative two-by-two block with private
   internal glues. Mechanism sketch, not a complete proofreading compiler.
5. Spatial growth mismatch, weak facet initiation and seed-independent nucleation;
   distinguish physical event and denominator.
6. Calibrated observation confusion channel: both false positives and false
   negatives, with a numerical inversion example and identifiability boundary.

Every plate has editable vector source and a semantic TXT specification.
Recognition interfaces are not covalent bonds. Color is supplemented by position,
labels and line style.
