# Chapter 6 storyboard — approved author scope, 19 September 2026

Question: how much duplex is present, rather than whether two strings complement?
The model is ideal, dilute, equilibrium A+B=AB with distinct complementary
strands. Nearest-neighbor calculations are restricted to canonical perfectly
matched non-self-complementary duplexes in the Table 2 1 M NaCl convention.
No wet-lab validation, kinetic inference, mismatch model, or salt extrapolation.

1. Molecular inventory: oriented single strands and antiparallel duplex; solid
   backbones, dashed pairing, equilibrium arrows; conserved strand totals.
2. Free-energy accounting: opposing enthalpic and entropic contributions with
   dimensioned axes, plus standard-state versus actual concentration distinction.
3. Duplex fraction curves generated from mass balance; exact 100/100/5 nM
   example and ligand-excess approximation as a labeled negative control.
4. Nearest-neighbor geometry: adjacent paired layers and two end corrections;
   dinucleotide parameters are oriented duplex steps, not isolated pair energies.
5. Melting curves generated from the declared parameter model; concentration
   shifts, half-bound level, and a scope strip for excluded folds/mismatches.

Every figure has a semantic TXT companion. Every numerical table/curve is
generated from tested reference code. Original LaTeX prose follows the storyboard.
