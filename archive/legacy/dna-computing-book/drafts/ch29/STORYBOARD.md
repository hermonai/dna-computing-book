# Chapter 29 storyboard

Each of the six figures has an editable TeX source and a semantic TXT companion. The manuscript captions define the figure's claim and limitations.

1. Chemical-model/program/observation boundary, with forward/backward arrows and units.
2. Computed population and analytical sensitivities on separate axes.
3. Continuous vs discrete derivative targets and detached-state falsifier.
4. Nucleotide simplex, final probabilities, discrete candidate and GC counterexample.
5. Proposal-to-validation loop with explicit failure feedback.
6. Four failure signatures and their independent remedies.

Palette is shared with prior chapters. Labels, line styles, values and layout carry semantics without depending only on color. No ASCII art or published artwork is used.
