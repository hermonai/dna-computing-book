# Chapter 11: Sequence design and graph encoding

Author-approved storyboard, 24 September 2026. Approval means an author design decision, not independent scientific review.

Scope: original implementation-first chapter; preserve all prior chapters and accepted editions. Each plate is authored as editable TikZ with a semantic TXT companion. Computed labels come from tested reference artifacts. Inspect print-size color and grayscale renderings.

1. **01-polarity**: Two strands aligned antiparallel, with literal bases. Compare same written direction versus physical pairing. Caption: reverse-complementing is a representation transform, not an extra molecular operation. Risk: polarity and backbone/pairing confusion.

2. **02-offset**: Two distinct equal-length words with high Hamming distance but a five-base shifted match to a reverse complement. Show the offset and paired segment; mismatch screen is not free energy.

3. **03-hairpin**: One strand folds to a paired stem and unpaired loop. Preserve covalent continuity and label terminal polarity. Exact inverted-repeat screen is a candidate warning, not a predicted equilibrium structure.

4. **04-graph**: A directed four-vertex graph and half-domain edge encodings. At the shared vertex, a complementary splint aligns adjacent edge oligos across one nick. Distinguish graph arrows, base pairing and ligation.

5. **05-seam**: A target word absent from either isolated domain appears across their concatenation seam. Enumerate full-construct windows. Risk: validating parts does not validate compositions.

Acceptance: derive the governing relationships, test independently solvable limits and counterexamples, include worked exercises and primary-source access notes. Review source/PDF hashes; cumulative integration and independent specialist/reader review remain open.
