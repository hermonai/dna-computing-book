SEMANTIC GRAPH — Concept-to-observation identity hierarchy

Purpose: Explain the mechanism and its scope; companion to the editable TikZ plate.
Notation: prose nodes and typed relationships, not ASCII art.

NODES
N0: Design v4: abstract mechanism.
N1: Two sequence sets: corrected base may preserve concept but changes realization.
N2: Material lot 7: a specific acquisition/production provenance.
N3: Preparations 11,12,13: explicitly named physical units.
N4: Observation fields: nested images or reads.

RELATIONSHIPS
R0: Design -> sequence set: one-to-many physical realization.
R1: Sequence set -> lot: sequence identity does not identify material batch.
R2: Lot -> independently prepared unit: separate preparation identity.
R3: Preparation -> fields: repeat observations share upstream variation.

READING AND LIMITS
- Drawing is schematic, not an experimental workflow or chemical structure.
- Independent status must follow intervention assignment and actual preparation process.
- Extra images do not create independent preparation replicates.

Visual encoding: navy/blue denotes state and bound evidence; green denotes valid flow or independent unit; red denotes failure or limitation. Text and arrow roles preserve meaning in grayscale.
