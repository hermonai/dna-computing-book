D22 — DETERMINISTIC SEQUENCE REDUNDANCY VS PHYSICAL FUNCTION
Scope: separates conditional Shannon information from the molecular utility of a complementary strand.

LEGEND
  ┌─┐ information object   → deterministic relation   ╔═╗ physical function domain
   ⇒ mathematical consequence   ⊣ invalid inference

┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ RANDOM STRAND S⁺            │ distribution over Σ_DNA^n
└──────────────┬───────────────┘
               │ deterministic bijection S⁻ = RC(S⁺)
               ▼
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ COMPLEMENTARY SEQUENCE S⁻   │
└──────────────┬───────────────┘
               ⇒ H(S⁻ | S⁺) = 0 and H(S⁺,S⁻) = H(S⁺)
               ▼
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ SEQUENCE-ONLY CONCLUSION     │ the ideal second sequence adds no uncertainty
│ when the first is known      │
└──────────────┬───────────────┘
               │ does not erase the second molecule
               ▼
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ PHYSICAL ROLES OF TWO STRANDS                                                       ║
║ templating · selective recognition · reversible association · structural stability ║
║ strand-specific binding · access control · damage detection/repair in named systems ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

BOUNDARY RULES
  zero conditional sequence entropy ⊣ “the second strand is physically useless.”
  two physical copies              ⊣ a universal error-correcting code without a repair model.

READING NOTES
1. Information is defined relative to random variables and what is already known.
2. Physical function depends on embodiment, dynamics, and interacting machinery, not sequence entropy alone.
3. Mismatches, modifications, and damage can make the observed second strand non-deterministic under a richer model.
