# Chapter 27 visual teaching plan

## 01-ladder

A conceptual strand/complex drawing maps to the declared universe (A,B,C,AB,ABC), then x=(0,0,1,1,0). Complex AB counts as one object. Hidden domains, conformation, orientation and space are omitted, not proven irrelevant.

## 02-rule

Reaction 2A+E -> B+E requires both A multiplicity and catalyst E. 3A+E becomes A+B+E. 3A alone cannot fire despite E having zero net change. Shapes depict conceptual species, not a validated enzyme mechanism.

## 03-graph

Diamond vertices A+B+C, AB+C, AC+B, ABC. Labels bind_AB/bind_C and bind_AC/bind_B join at ABC. Four states and four edges; one predecessor tree cannot replace the labeled graph.

## 04-bfs

Depth-bounded: complete within K but outside successors exist. Closed set: whole queue processed, all successors inside, global absence provable. Budget exhausted: completeness false, outside count unknown, negatives undecided. Closure can contain cycles.

## 05-invariant

Assembly unit weights (1,1,1,2,3) keep total three for A+B+C -> AB+C -> ABC. Left nullspace w^T S=0 proves an invariant on legal paths only; it does not prove enabledness or reachability.

## 06-boundary

Formal count rules ask reachability; quantitative models add rates/units/noise and ask probability/time; physical observations add molecular construction/assays/controls. Each adds new adequacy gates.

Editable TikZ sources and semantic TXT companions; labels and contrasting line/shape conventions preserve meaning in grayscale. No externally copied artwork.
