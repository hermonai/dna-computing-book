Primer binding geometry on two antiparallel templates. The forward primer starts at coordinate a and grows rightward; the reverse primer begins at b and grows leftward. Both grow at their 3-prime ends. The reverse primer sequence GTA is written 5-prime to 3-prime, while its display under the upper template reads ATG from left to right. Dashed vertical lines locate the intended boundaries, not chemical cuts. Three-base primers and a ten-base target are coordinate teaching objects, not usable assay designs.
