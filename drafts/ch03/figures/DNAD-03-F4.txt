DNAD-03-F4: A path as a Boolean assignment

QUESTION
What must the SAT encoding forbid?

OBJECTS
Vertex-position matrix
row/column constraints
forbidden transitions

RELATION / MECHANISM
Choose one vertex per slot and one slot per vertex; impose endpoints and legal adjacency

INFERENCE
Satisfying assignments and fixed-endpoint paths correspond exactly.

BOUNDARY
Reducing Hamiltonian path to SAT alone does not prove Hamiltonian path hard.

SOURCE
Original CNF encoding and exhaustive tests
