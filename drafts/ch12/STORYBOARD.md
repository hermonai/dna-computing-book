# Chapter 12: Generate--filter--verify algorithms

Author-approved storyboard, 24 September 2026. Approval means an author design decision, not independent scientific review.

Scope: original implementation-first chapter; preserve all prior chapters and accepted editions. Each plate is authored as editable TikZ with a semantic TXT companion. Computed labels come from tested reference artifacts. Inspect print-size color and grayscale renderings.

1. **01-pool**: A four-vertex directed graph alongside a multiset of walks; marks track endpoint, length, coverage and adjacency. Cardinality counts copies separately from distinct paths.

2. **02-capture**: A tethered complementary probe captures a target-containing strand; a partial off-target can also associate and a true strand can be lost. Show bead, tether, paired domain, wash/release outcomes; no recipe or perfect-specificity implication.

3. **03-logic**: Truth table on four literal path witnesses, including wrong direction, repeated vertex and missing endpoint. Proof requires generation and filtering contracts independently.

4. **04-loss**: Computed probability of retaining at least one true copy versus starting copy count. Distinguish expected count from nonempty probability; independence and fixed efficiencies are explicit.

5. **05-order**: Two finite-capacity filter orders have different retained identities; draw occupied slots and original molecule IDs. Contrast commuting ideal diagonal masks with order-dependent admission.
