Question: What exactly does one XOR parity equation recover?
Objects: equal-width binary vectors A=1010, B=0110 and P=A XOR B=1100. Arrows from both source vectors feed the parity vector, not one another.
Erasure trace: when A is absent and B/P are valid, B XOR P=1010 reconstructs A. Any two correct distinct-address shards determine the third.
Failure branches: three present shards with changed P=1101 have syndrome 0001, so the reference rejects without guessing which shard is wrong. Two erased shards leave one equation and multiple source pairs.
Assumptions: known addresses, equal widths and independently validated available frames. Single parity has minimum distance two and is not an arbitrary error-correcting code.
