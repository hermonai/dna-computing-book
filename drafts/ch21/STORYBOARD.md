# Chapter 21 storyboard
Author plan, 27 September 2026; independent review remains open.

Question: what makes an analog concentration implement a composable digital gate?
Start from two uncertain inputs, not ideal bits. Prerequisites: strand exchange
(19) and CRN semantics/inventory (20). Chapter 22 owns spatial self-assembly.

1. Threshold capture: polarity-correct single strand plus complementary sink
   becomes a duplex. Continuous covalent backbones and pairing marks are distinct;
   finite sink inventory is consumed. No invented chemical reset is implied.
2. Transfer curve: generated threshold/restoration response with low/high
   acceptance bands; annotate uncertainty in the threshold, finite fuel and time.
3. Timing race: threshold capture and catalytic release compete for the same
   free input. A computed false-positive transient defeats the staged model.
4. Fan-out: finite output inventory is partitioned among three receiving loads;
   distinguish dividing a sample, consuming a signal, and catalytic sequestration.
5. Feedback phase line: autocatalytic conversion of finite fuel, fixed points and
   seed-dependent trajectories; regeneration is not bistability or free reset.

Reference: staged exact transfer, interval certification, exact concurrent-race
solution checked by RK4, resource/load accounting and logistic feedback.
Prove an interval composition theorem with declared read windows and private
resources; provide counterexamples when the assumptions fail. Twelve worked
exercises. Five editable TikZ figures plus semantic TXT companions.
