SEMANTIC GRAPH — Observation-to-classification boundary

Purpose: Explain the mechanism and its scope; companion to the editable TikZ plate.
Notation: prose nodes and typed relationships, not ASCII art.

NODES
N0: Original raw images/traces/reads and calibration.
N1: Versioned mapping: selection, segmentation, QC, threshold, denominator.
N2: Derived per-object and per-preparation variables.
N3: Coded identities at required observation-freeze boundary.

RELATIONSHIPS
R0: Raw+calibration -> mapping -> derived results.
R1: Mapping revision -> new analytical artifact, retained original evidence.
R2: Observation freeze -> documented later unblinding permitted by minimal contract.

READING AND LIMITS
- Particle sketches are illustrative, not raw microscopy.
- Generic accuracy conflates distinct denominators.
- Real studies may require stricter blinding and authenticated code custody.

Visual encoding: navy/blue denotes state and bound evidence; green denotes valid flow or independent unit; red denotes failure or limitation. Text and arrow roles preserve meaning in grayscale.
