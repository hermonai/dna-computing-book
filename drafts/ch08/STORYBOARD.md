# Chapter 8 storyboard

The chapter owns substrate transformation, not a wet-lab protocol. Figures are original TikZ mechanisms with semantic TXT descriptions; molecular identity, polarity, bond state, and count changes must remain traceable.

1. EcoRI recognition and two staggered cuts: paired GAATTC/CTTAAG, separate backbone cut sites, resulting four-base 5-prime overhang. Show recognition versus cleavage, not scissor decoration. Risk: confusing a displayed bottom strand with its 5-to-3 sequence.
2. Nick sealing: three chemical activation stages, enzyme-AMP then DNA-AMP then continuous backbone; template pairing stays intact. Show 3-OH and 5-phosphate; released AMP is not incorporated as a base. Protein outline schematic, not an atomistic structure.
3. Primer extension: one template-aligned nucleotide added at the primer 3-end, dNTP inventory decreases, pyrophosphate released. Contrast a nick with a missing nucleotide gap. Synthesis and template-reading directions explicitly opposed.
4. Catalytic occupancy and turnover: E+S, ES, E+P with distinct binding, dissociation, and catalytic arrows. Generated substrate-rate curve distinguishes Km from Kd and rate from yield.
5. Transient occupancy: generated full mass-action and clamped-substrate oracle curves expose the initial boundary layer; enzyme and substrate conservation must survive integration. No assigned rate is an enzyme measurement.

Each plate supports a derivation or a code invariant. Use labels and line styles in grayscale. Review every page and inspect all figures at final size.
