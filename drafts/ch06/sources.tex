\section*{Original derivations and pedagogical reference}
The mass-balance solver, numerical-stability derivation, counterexamples,
sequence arithmetic, figures, and exercises are original teaching artifacts.
Raschka's author companion overview and chapter map were inspected:
\url{https://www.sebastianraschka.com/llms-from-scratch/}.
We use its general implementation-first progression, not copied prose or
artwork. The full commercial book was not inspected.

Computed values are model outputs, not measurements. Author technical,
editorial, and visual checks do not replace independent scientific review
or a reader study.
