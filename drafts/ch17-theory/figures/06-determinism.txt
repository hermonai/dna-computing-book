FIGURE: Prefix conflict versus reachable ambiguity
QUESTION: What must a syntactic determinism check compare?
UPPER LABELS: a is a prefix of ab.
LOWER LABELS: a is a prefix of ab.
RULE 1: From s consume upper a and lower a; enter f.
RULE 2: From s consume upper ab and lower ab; enter g.
WITNESS: At initial state s, unread pair ab/ab is admissible under identity
pairing and enables both rules. Their possible eventual acceptance is a
different question from their simultaneous availability.
LIMIT: A local conflict elsewhere may be unreachable. This checker is not
a decision procedure for weak determinism.
DESIGN: Shared-prefix segments have matching geometry and letter labels;
the unmatched b extension is distinguished by position, text and color.
