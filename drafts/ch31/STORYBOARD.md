# Chapter 31 storyboard — authored before the rewrite

Question: when does a molecular implementation support its advertised computation?

1. Stack: specification, kinetic realization, observation and context-specific claim.
2. Strand mechanism: exposed toehold, antiparallel domain pairing, branch migration and release; schematic, not a sequence design.
3. Kinetics: three elementary reactions, stoichiometry and two conserved pools; held-fuel exact transient versus a reduced model.
4. Composition: competing capture hazards and propagated perturbation; loading changes the interface.
5. Environment: degradation and reporter activation are separate pathways; cell compartments are not a quality ranking.
6. Evidence: independent experimental units, gain/offset intervals and a context-matching gate, with negative observations retained.

All six plates have editable TikZ and semantic TXT descriptions. Computed traces are model outputs, not measurements. Twenty worked questions connect derivations, implementations and falsifiers.
