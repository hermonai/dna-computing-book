# Chapter 30 figure storyboard

1. Expression: antiparallel DNA, cis region, trans factor, polymerase, nascent
   RNA, export and ribosome. Distinct carriers; geometry is not a scale model.
2. Context: nucleosomes and candidate enhancer/promoter contact, then two
   alleles sharing a trans pool. Contact alone does not establish regulation.
3. Circuit: repression bars expose the two-gene mechanism; nullclines and
   marked fixed points distinguish stable branches from the saddle.
   Generated trajectories show slow symmetry breaking.
4. Development: recorded ancestry and snapshot pseudotime use the same three
   records but different relations. Daughter counts are complementary.
5. Evolution: explicit 1+3 parent counts become 2+3 descendants under assigned
   reproductive weights. Mutation follows reproduction; regulation is distinct.
6. Mapping: physical carrier, observation and recurrence have separate interfaces,
   explicitly omitting chemistry, inheritance and causal measurement.
