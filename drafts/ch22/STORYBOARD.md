# Chapter 22 figure storyboard

All six figures are editable TikZ with semantic UTF-8 descriptions. These are
original explanatory constructions, not experimental micrographs or atomic models.

1. **Local attachment contract.** Two independent south/west contacts give total
   strength two. Glue strength is an abstract integer, not a measured energy.
2. **Computed XOR product.** The eight-by-eight interior is generated from the
   tested reference result; a strength-two L seed is distinct from computation.
3. **Ambiguous frontier.** Identical input glues admit two different output bits
   after adding the mutant; its identity is not merely a renamed correct tile.
4. **Sticky-end association.** Complementary, antiparallel ends associate. Two
   nicks remain unligated; this interface sketch is not a complete DX tile.
5. **Competing kinetic exits.** Detachment competes with locking of a wrong
   attachment. The conditional probability is a teaching model, not measured yield.
6. **Origami routing abstraction.** One scaffold and continuous antiparallel
   staples constrain a designed route. Helical phase and three-dimensional
   geometry are omitted and cannot be inferred from the domain diagram.

Palette: Blue #173E60, Green #26714D, Red #AA354C, Pale #EDF4F8,
Gold #F2D799. Inspect color and grayscale pages at print size, especially glue
labels, strand polarity, competing-exit arrows, and the ambiguity panel.
