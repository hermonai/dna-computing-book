# Chapter 5 production storyboard

Author-approved scope, 15 September 2026: DNA chemistry and sequence geometry.
Keep accepted and earlier review sources unchanged. Author prose directly in LaTeX.

1. Nucleotide topology: numbered deoxyribose ring, base attachment and 3'/5'
   connection sites; distinguish connectivity from stereochemistry and physical size.
2. Phosphodiester path: two sugars linked through 3'-O-P-O-5'; identify the
   covalent path and distinguish a nucleotide residue from an isolated dNTP.
3. Oriented duplex: ACGTA above TGCAT; show the lower strand's 5'-to-3' reading
   order TACGT without changing the molecule. Labels and arrow semantics mandatory.
4. Pairing versus stacking: complementary partners across a layer versus adjacent
   layers along the helix; no invented free energies or bond lengths.
5. Interval reversal: length-nine strip, [2,6) mapped to [3,7); reverse both
   coordinates and sequence content. Show boundary ticks, not ambiguous base indices.

Ambiguity-set complementation uses an exact table. Hairpin/palindrome discussion
will distinguish a symbolic relation from a thermodynamic structure prediction.
Every plate gets a semantic TXT description and a self-contained limiting caption.
Tests exhaust short sequence spaces and independently check coordinate identities.
