\section*{Original calculations and access boundaries}
The closed-form reversible-binding derivation, implementation, numerical
convergence experiment, finite-pool detailed-balance oracle, one-pair
counterexample, and figures are original teaching artifacts.
No published experimental trajectory or benchmark is reproduced.
The stated rates are illustrative inputs. The whole Gillespie paper's
experimental examples were not reviewed for this chapter.

The author companion to Sebastian Raschka's
\emph{Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch)} informed the general
implementation-first progression:
\url{https://www.sebastianraschka.com/llms-from-scratch/}.
Its overview and chapter map were inspected; the full commercial book
was not. No prose, code listings, or artwork were copied.

The chapter distinguishes exactness within a mathematical model from
physical validity, and author checks from independent scientific review.
The latter, reader feedback, and cumulative integration remain open.
