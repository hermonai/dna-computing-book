# DNAD-24 storyboard

1. Resource path: input size, population inventory, serial dependencies and
   verified readout; arrows are accounting dependencies, not a wet-lab recipe.
2. Occupancy: eight identity classes, ten copies, six represented identities;
   species coverage is visibly different from molecule abundance.
3. Equal inventory and volume: two versus eight species, one partner's
   concentration and encounter time; fourfold change uses assigned kinetics.
4. Completion curves: computed Erlang four-stage CDF and independent all-chain
   laws for one, ten and one hundred jobs. Solid/dashed styles survive grayscale.
5. Readout gates: witness inventory, aliquot, detection and finite report channel;
   probability of missing every witness is not classifier calibration.
6. Pareto comparison: freeze success/deadline/observation, account for both designs,
   then decide dominance or incomparability without invented device measurements.

Each source is editable TikZ with a same-stem TXT semantic description.
