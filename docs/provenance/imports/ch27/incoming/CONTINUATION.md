# Continuity
DNAD-26 required explicit experiment states, identities, observations and evidence.
DNAD-27 creates the analogous contract for symbolic molecular simulation. A simulator
state is a finite multiset of typed symbolic species; a rule consumes and produces
stoichiometric multisets; applicability, successor generation, conservation laws,
bounded search and acceptance are explicit. Symbolic reachability is not a kinetic
prediction and not evidence that a physical implementation works. DNAD-28 adds time,
rates and stochastic trajectories.
