# Continuity
DNAD-19–22 established strand displacement, CRNs, circuits and assembly; DNAD-23–26 established reliability/evidence practice; DNAD-27–29 established symbolic, kinetic and differentiable models; DNAD-30 restored the biology/computation boundary. DNAD-31 asks what modern molecular-programming systems actually demonstrate. Formal compilation, simulation, sequence design, cell-free experiment, serum/cell experiment and in-vivo function are different evidence levels. A successful reporter trace supports only the variables and conditions measured. This chapter supplies an executable claim ledger rather than a new wet-lab protocol.
