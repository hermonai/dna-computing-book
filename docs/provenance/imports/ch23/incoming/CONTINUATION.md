# Continuity contract
DNAD-22 defined oriented tile types, tau=2 attachment, producibility, terminality,
directedness, a deterministic XOR/Sierpiński construction, ambiguity, finite area,
and the boundary between abstract glue strengths and molecular energetics.

DNAD-23 preserves those definitions and adds:
- error event, defect, error rate, object yield and observation error;
- independent-site and correlated finite-size reliability models;
- odd repetition decoding and exact binomial failure probability;
- proofreading as a structured cooperative construction rather than a magic
  post-processing decoder;
- growth error versus seed/facet nucleation versus kinetic trap;
- controls and confidence intervals for physical reliability experiments.

The chapter does not claim that its independent-error equations describe a specific
wet-lab system. Reported experimental numbers are attributed to primary literature.
