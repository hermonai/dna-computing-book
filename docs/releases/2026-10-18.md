# Undergraduate textbook review edition — 18 October 2026

DNA Computing: Molecular mechanisms, algorithms and evidence.

This public **prerelease** is the undergraduate textbook revision of the complete
manuscript: 32 chapters in eight parts, a Preface and reading guide, an Epilogue
on research frontiers and open problems, three appendices (molecular biology
primer, mathematical toolkit, timeline 1953–2026), learning goals and key ideas
in every chapter, one unified bibliography of 123 cited works, and
39 new or restyled illustrations in a shared visual language. New material
includes a DNA primer (Chapter 1), skeletal nucleotide and base-pair structures
(Chapter 5), DNA neural networks from seesaw gates to in-vitro supervised
learning (Chapter 21) and algorithmic self-assembly at scale (Chapter 22).

It is an author-agent review edition, not an independently accepted scientific
publication. The earlier prerelease `dna-review-2026-10-17` is unchanged.

## Remaining publication gates

Full-resolution page-by-page visual review, independent specialist review of the
new chemistry, neural-network, origami and frontier material, classroom or reader
testing, accessibility tagging, completion of abbreviated author lists in some
2025–2026 references, and two planned figures (a Chapter 30 molecular-recorder
plate and a redrawn Chapter 32 map). Computed model outputs are not laboratory
measurements or evidence of industrial or genomic capability.
