\BookPart{Modelling and simulation}{modelling}{%
Before a strand is ordered, a molecular program is usually simulated. Part~VII
builds three complementary kinds of model from scratch: symbolic simulators
that enumerate what can happen, kinetic and stochastic simulators that predict
how fast and how variably it happens, and differentiable models that let
optimisation algorithms tune rates and sequences. Each simulator is tested
against exact solutions, and each is placed honestly beside the experiments it
can and cannot replace.

\textbf{Chapters in this part}
\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
\item \textbf{27\quad Symbolic molecular simulation.} How do we enumerate every reachable molecular state, and what does a found pathway prove?
\item \textbf{28\quad Kinetic and stochastic simulation.} How do ordinary differential equations and the Gillespie algorithm predict molecular dynamics?
\item \textbf{29\quad Differentiable molecular and sequence models.} How can gradients guide the design of rates and sequences, and when do they mislead?
\end{itemize}}
