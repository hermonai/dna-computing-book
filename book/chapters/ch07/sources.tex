\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Gillespie's 1977 paper introduced the exact stochastic simulation algorithm
implemented in this chapter.\cite{Gillespie1977} Higham's tutorial review is an
accessible introduction to deterministic and stochastic models of chemical
reactions and their numerical simulation.\cite{Higham2008} Chapter~28 extends
both descriptions to whole reaction networks.
