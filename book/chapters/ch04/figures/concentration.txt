Question: What changes when a fixed molecular inventory occupies a smaller volume?
Invariant: Both populations have 602,214,076 specified molecules.
States: Volume 1 microlitre at 1 nanomolar; volume 0.1 microlitre at 10 nanomolar.
Relation: Concentration equals molecule count divided by Avogadro constant and volume.
Inference: Inventory and concentration are not interchangeable quantities.
Boundary: Six glyphs represent each population; tube dimensions and fill levels are not quantitative scale drawings.
