\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Seelig and colleagues built the first enzyme-free nucleic-acid logic
circuits,\cite{Seelig2006} and Qian and Winfree scaled them up with the seesaw
gate.\cite{QianWinfree2011} The neural-network line runs from Qian, Winfree and
Bruck's associative memory\cite{QianWinfreeBruck2011} through Cherry and Qian's
winner-take-all classifier\cite{CherryQian2018} to supervised learning in
vitro.\cite{CherryQian2025} Xiong and colleagues built convolutional
architectures,\cite{Xiong2022} Lv and colleagues programmable gate
arrays,\cite{Lv2023} and Song and Qian heat-rechargeable circuits.\cite{SongQian2025}
Zhang and colleagues applied a DNA classifier to clinical serum
samples.\cite{Zhang2020cancer}
