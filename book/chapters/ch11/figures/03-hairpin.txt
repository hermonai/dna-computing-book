Figure: an inverted repeat allows a hairpin.
Panel A: one strand 5'-GCG AAA CGC-3'; the first three bases (a stem arm) are the
reverse complement of the last three.
Panel B: the strand folds so that G pairs with C, C with G and G with C in a
three-base-pair stem, with an unpaired AAA loop; one continuous backbone runs
around the loop. No energy or population estimate is implied.
