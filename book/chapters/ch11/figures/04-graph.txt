Figure: a shared vertex at an edge junction.
Panel A: a graph with vertices A, B, C and D, edges A to B, A to C, B to D,
C to D, and both B to C and C to B. B is shared by the edges A to B and B to C.
Panel B: the molecules at B. The edge strand for A to B reads TCAG CCTA (R_A
followed by L_B) and the edge strand for B to C reads GTCA GATC (R_B followed by
L_C), both 5' to 3'. A splint complementary to the B code CCTA GTCA runs
antiparallel underneath, pairing GGAT with CCTA and CAGT with GTCA, and holds the
two edge strands end to end across a nick. Ligation, not pairing, would seal the
backbone.
