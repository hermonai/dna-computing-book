Figure: one duplex, two stored strings.
Panel A: a physical duplex with the top strand 5'-AACG-3' paired base by base to
the bottom strand, which runs 3' to 5' and reads TTGC from left to right. Grey
rungs are base pairs; the coloured lines are backbones with 3' half-arrowheads.
Panel B: both strands recorded in the 5'-to-3' convention: top AACG, bottom
CGTT. The bottom string is the reverse complement, rc(AACG) = CGTT. Writing the
bottom strand in its own direction changes the description, not the pairing.
