Figure: a fixed comparison can hide a shifted pairing.
Panel A: the expected partner rc(ACGTAC) = GTACGT is compared position by
position with the given strand TACGTA, both written 5' to 3'; all six positions
differ.
Panel B: the same strands drawn as a duplex, with the given strand running 3' to
5' and shifted by one position: five consecutive positions are complementary
and one base overhangs at each end. The geometry is a warning, not a
binding-yield prediction.
