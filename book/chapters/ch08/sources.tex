\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Manufacturers' technical pages define the substrate requirements assumed in
this chapter for EcoRI,\cite{NEBEcoRI} T4 DNA ligase\cite{NEBT4Ligase} and T4 DNA
polymerase.\cite{NEBT4Pol} The crystal structure of T4 DNA ligase shows how the
enzyme engages a nicked duplex.\cite{Shi2018} The IUBMB recommendations on
enzyme kinetics define the steady-state and transient terminology used
here.\cite{IUBMB1981} Benenson and colleagues' molecular automaton (Chapter~17)
is an elegant example of restriction and ligation used as computational
steps.\cite{Benenson2001}
