\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
SantaLucia's unified nearest-neighbour parameters are the basis of the energy
model used here and of most DNA design software.\cite{SantaLucia1998} The
NUPACK documentation explains the physical model, ensembles and conditions
behind modern secondary-structure prediction,\cite{NUPACKmodel} and the NUPACK
paper describes the software itself.\cite{Zadeh2011} For the distinction between
stacking and pairing contributions, see Yakovchuk and colleagues.\cite{Yakovchuk2006}
