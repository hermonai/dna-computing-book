\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Addgene's protocol is a practical introduction to agarose gel
electrophoresis,\cite{AddgeneGel} and the manufacturer's technical note explains
common sources of unexpected bands.\cite{NEBBands} The Qubit assay guide
illustrates how a fluorescence measurement is calibrated against standards
before it reports a concentration.\cite{ThermoQubit} The NHGRI glossary gives a
short definition of electrophoresis.\cite{NHGRIElectrophoresis}
