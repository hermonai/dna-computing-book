\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
The FAIR principles state how scientific data should be findable, accessible,
interoperable and reusable.\cite{Wilkinson2016} The DNA origami methods primer by
Dey and colleagues describes design, assembly, purification and characterisation
practice for a widely used molecular system.\cite{Dey2021} Organick and
colleagues' random-access storage study is a good example of reporting a
complete write--read workflow.\cite{Organick2018}
