\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Sipser's textbook covers Turing machines, universality and the two-stack
construction.\cite{Sipser2013} Bennett's review of the thermodynamics of
computation describes a hypothetical enzymatic Turing machine and discusses
the energy cost of reversible computation.\cite{Bennett1982} Soloveichik,
Seelig and Winfree showed that DNA strand displacement can implement arbitrary
chemical reaction networks,\cite{Soloveichik2010} which connects universality
results for reaction networks (Chapter~20) to molecular realisations. The
scaffolded DNA computer is a recent bounded experimental
architecture.\cite{Sterin2026}
