\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Church, Gao and Kosuri\cite{Church2012} and Goldman and colleagues\cite{Goldman2013}
reported early large-scale encodings; Erlich and Zielinski introduced fountain
codes for DNA storage;\cite{ErlichZielinski2017} Organick and colleagues
demonstrated random access at scale.\cite{Organick2018} Ceze, Nivala and Strauss
review the field.\cite{Ceze2019} Chen and colleagues quantified molecular
bias,\cite{Chen2020bias} Gimpel and colleagues built a digital twin of the error
channel,\cite{Gimpel2023} Bar-Lev and colleagues combined coding theory with deep
learning,\cite{BarLev2025} and Li and colleagues developed bijective constrained
coding.\cite{Li2026} Zhang and colleagues write data as epigenetic marks on
premade DNA,\cite{ZhangC2024} and Kosuri and Church review the synthesis
technologies that limit writing.\cite{Kosuri2014}
