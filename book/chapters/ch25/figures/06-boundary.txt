Figure: storage and computation share engineering but have different goals.
Left: storage preserves a message. A file u is encoded into strands, passes a
noisy channel and is decoded to u-hat; success means u-hat equals u or a declared
failure, and cost is counted per stored bit.
Right: computation transforms a state. An input molecular state x is acted on by
designed dynamics F and read out as an output state y; success means the correct
output, read out correctly, and cost is counted per computation.
Beneath: shared engineering (sequence constraints, molecule inventory,
addressing, assembly and readout errors, independent physical validation).
Storage density is not a computational throughput benchmark.
