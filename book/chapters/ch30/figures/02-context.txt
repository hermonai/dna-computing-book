Genomic context and two-allele control. A folded chromatin fiber brings a distal enhancer candidate toward a promoter while an intervening locus remains along the contour. Circles denote schematic nucleosomes; the dashed contact is not a bond or evidence of causality. Two separate allele fibers share the same diffusible trans factor x but can have different cis rate c_i and accessibility q_i. The multiplicative model is stipulated and not universal.
