\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Soloveichik, Seelig and Winfree showed how DNA strand displacement can
implement arbitrary chemical reaction networks.\cite{Soloveichik2010} Chen, Doty
and Soloveichik characterised the functions that reaction networks compute
deterministically,\cite{Chen2014} and Kini and Doty study robustness to
reversible reactions.\cite{KiniDoty2026} On the experimental side, Chen and
colleagues built programmable chemical controllers from DNA,\cite{Chen2013} and
Srinivas and colleagues built an enzyme-free oscillator from a compiled reaction
network.\cite{Srinivas2017} Cardelli's work on process rate semantics clarifies
how rate conventions should be stated.\cite{Cardelli2008}
