\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Seeman's 1982 paper proposed branched DNA junctions as building blocks for
designed lattices,\cite{Seeman1982} and Winfree and colleagues realised
two-dimensional crystals of DNA tiles.\cite{Winfree1998} Winfree's thesis
founded algorithmic self-assembly,\cite{Winfree1998thesis} Rothemund and Winfree
analysed its program-size complexity,\cite{RothemundWinfree2000} and Doty's
review surveys the theory.\cite{Doty2012} Rothemund, Papadakis and Winfree grew
Sierpinski triangles,\cite{Rothemund2004} Rothemund introduced DNA
origami,\cite{Rothemund2006} and Ke and colleagues built three-dimensional shapes
from DNA bricks.\cite{Ke2012} Evans and Winfree review the physical principles of
tile assembly.\cite{EvansWinfree2017} For recent advances see Woods and
colleagues\cite{Woods2019} and Evans and colleagues.\cite{Evans2024}
