\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Watson and Crick's one-page paper proposing the double helix is still worth
reading for its economy.\cite{WatsonCrick1953} The IUPAC--IUB recommendations
define atom numbering and chain direction,\cite{IUPAC1983,IUPAC1970} and the
INSDC feature-table appendix defines the ambiguity codes used in sequence
databases.\cite{INSDC} The National Human Genome Research Institute fact sheet is
a concise introduction to DNA's components,\cite{NHGRIDNA} and the chapter on
DNA structure in \emph{Molecular Biology of the Cell} gives the textbook
background.\cite{Alberts2002} Yakovchuk, Protozanova and Frank-Kamenetskii
separated the stacking and pairing contributions to duplex
stability.\cite{Yakovchuk2006}
