Figure: a conditional write implements AND over a population.
Each row starts with five copies of a three-address memory strand (addresses a,
b and c from left to right; a green sticker marks a 1 on a or b). The four input
registers are 000, 010, 100 and 110. The population is separated on a, then on b;
only the tube with a = 1 and b = 1 receives an amber sticker on the output
address c. After merging, the registers are 000, 010, 100 and 111, five copies
each. The output address is cleared first; all twenty backbones return and none
is copied.
