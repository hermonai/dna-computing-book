Figure: comparing two designs under one fixed contract.
A box at the top fixes the contract: input, correct-output event, failure
tolerance, deadline and observation. Two designs, A and B, are shown as
schematic resource profiles (bars for species S, copies N, depth d, readout and
energy E; the heights are illustrative, not data). A is cheaper on some axes and
B on others. A box beneath states the comparison rule: compare complete resource
vectors, including auxiliary species and failed runs; A dominates B only if no
cost is worse and at least one is better. Otherwise report the trade-off rather
than inventing a single winner.
