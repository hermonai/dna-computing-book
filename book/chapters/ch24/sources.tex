\section*{Further reading}

Boneh and colleagues analysed what DNA filtering models can compute and at what
cost.\cite{Boneh1996} Landauer's paper on irreversibility and heat
generation\cite{Landauer1961} and Bennett's review of the thermodynamics of
computation\cite{Bennett1982} set out the physical limits discussed in the
energy section. The SI definition of the mole underlies all conversions from
probability to matter.\cite{BIPMmole} For a modern resource account of a
thermodynamically driven computer, see St\'erin and colleagues.\cite{Sterin2026}
