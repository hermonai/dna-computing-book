\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Sipser's textbook gives the standard treatment of finite automata, regular
languages and the pumping-style argument used here.\cite{Sipser2013} Benenson
and colleagues built an autonomous finite automaton from DNA and
enzymes,\cite{Benenson2001} and Stojanovic and Stefanovic built a
deoxyribozyme-based automaton that plays tic-tac-toe.\cite{Stojanovic2003} The
scaffolded computer of St\'erin and colleagues simulates finite-state machines
on a DNA scaffold.\cite{Sterin2026}
