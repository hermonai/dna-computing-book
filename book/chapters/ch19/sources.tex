\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Yurke and colleagues' DNA-fuelled tweezers introduced toehold-mediated strand
displacement as a design tool,\cite{Yurke2000} and Zhang and Winfree measured how
toehold length and toehold exchange control its kinetics.\cite{ZhangWinfree2009}
Srinivas and colleagues explain the biophysics of the reaction with coarse-grained
simulations and a simple energy landscape.\cite{Srinivas2013} The review by Zhang
and Seelig surveys dynamic DNA nanotechnology built on this
mechanism,\cite{ZhangSeelig2011} and Wang and colleagues give design principles
for leakless displacement systems.\cite{Wang2018} Visual DSD is a widely used tool
for designing and simulating displacement circuits.\cite{Lakin2011}
