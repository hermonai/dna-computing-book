Figure: positive and negative evidence are asymmetric.
Panel A: a recovered strand carries the domains x1 = 1, x2 = 0 and x3 = 1. Decoding
it and evaluating every clause of the formula gives C1: x1 or x2 = 1 or 0 = 1;
C2: not x1 or x3 = 0 or 1 = 1; C3: not x2 or not x3 = 1 or 0 = 1. All three clauses
are satisfied, so the molecule certifies a satisfying assignment.
Panel B: an observation of no detected product points to two alternative
explanations: the formula is unsatisfiable, or a witness was never made, was
lost or was not detected. Only positive controls, loss estimates and detection
limits can separate these explanations.
