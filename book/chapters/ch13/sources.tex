\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Lipton's proposal introduced molecular satisfiability,\cite{Lipton1995} and
Braich and colleagues later solved a 20-variable 3-SAT instance with
gel-based capture modules, a careful example of the coverage and loss issues of
this chapter.\cite{Braich2002} Cook's theorem explains why satisfiability is the
canonical NP-complete problem.\cite{Cook1971} The scaffolded DNA computer of
St\'erin and colleagues explores a different architecture, in which constraints
are compiled into an energy landscape rather than into filters.\cite{Sterin2026}
