Figure 32.6: a map of the book's layers.
Four stacked bands: (A) molecular mechanism and resource accounting; (B)
algorithms, formal languages and molecular programs; (C) reliability, readout,
simulation and genomic organisation; (D) scoped evidence and abstraction.
Arrows run downward from each band to the next. A note states that each layer
depends on the one above it and that a claim made at the bottom must still be
tested against molecules.
