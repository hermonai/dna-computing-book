\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Czeizler, Czeizler, Kari and Salomaa study the descriptional complexity of
Watson--Crick automata, the formal model of this chapter.\cite{Czeizler2009}
Benenson and colleagues reported the autonomous biochemical automaton,\cite{Benenson2001}
then a version in which the input molecule also supplies the
fuel,\cite{Benenson2003} and Adar and colleagues made its transitions
stochastic.\cite{Adar2004} Benenson and colleagues later used a molecular
automaton to control gene expression logically in vitro.\cite{Benenson2004}
