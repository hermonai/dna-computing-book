Figure: four candidates and four tests.
Rows show candidate walks as chains of vertex chips: ABCD, ABBD, ABDC and ABD.
Columns test endpoints (start A, end D), length (four vertices), coverage (every
vertex present) and edges (every step is an edge of the graph A to B, A to C,
B to C, C to B, B to D, C to D). Green ticks pass and red crosses fail:
ABCD passes all four; ABBD passes endpoints and length but fails coverage and
edges (B to B is not an edge); ABDC fails endpoints and edges (D to C is not an
edge) but passes length and coverage; ABD passes endpoints and edges but fails
length and coverage. Only ABCD is a witness; no single column decides it.
