\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Adleman's experiment is the canonical generate--filter--verify
computation,\cite{Adleman1994} and Lipton showed how the same pattern handles
satisfiability.\cite{Lipton1995} Boneh, Dunworth, Lipton and Sgall analysed the
computational power of such filtering models.\cite{Boneh1996} Braich and
colleagues' 20-variable 3-SAT experiment is a demanding real-world test of the
loss and retention issues formalised here.\cite{Braich2002}
