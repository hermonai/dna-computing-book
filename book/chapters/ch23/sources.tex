\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Winfree and Bekbolatov introduced proofreading tile sets,\cite{WinfreeBekbolatov2004}
Chen, Schulman, Goel and Winfree reduced facet nucleation errors,\cite{Chen2007}
and Schulman, Wright and Winfree showed that redundancy reduces error rates
exponentially.\cite{Schulman2015} Evans and Winfree review the physics of tile
assembly errors.\cite{EvansWinfree2017} Shirt-Ediss and colleagues optimise origami
assembly by reducing off-target staple interactions.\cite{ShirtEdiss2026} The
21-algorithm tile set of Woods and colleagues reports per-tile error rates below
one in three thousand,\cite{Woods2019} a useful benchmark for this chapter's
budgets.
