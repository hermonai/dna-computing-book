Figure: reliability is an end-to-end contract, from design to reported label.
Four cards in a row: design C (tile set, sequences, rules); assembly A (a small
tile lattice in which one tile is wrong); signal Y (an instrument image or
trace); label D-hat (a classified decision, defect-free or defective). A
reaction arrow labelled P(A | C) links design to assembly, an observation arrow
labelled P(Y | A) links assembly to signal, and a data arrow labelled
P(D-hat | Y) links signal to label. Error sources are listed beneath: synthesis,
crosstalk, leak and growth faults change the molecules that exist; sampling,
instrument resolution and classification errors change what is reported. A
lower observed defect rate need not mean a lower molecular defect rate.
