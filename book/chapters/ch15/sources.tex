\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Kari and Ng introduce semi-simple splicing systems and their descriptional
complexity, a good entry point to the formal-language literature.\cite{KariNg2019}
For the engineering counterpart, the Golden Gate design guidance shows how Type
IIS enzymes and fusion overhangs order DNA fragments.\cite{NEBGoldenGate}
Chapter~8 develops the restriction and ligation chemistry that a splicing rule
abstracts.
