\section*{Further reading}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Further reading}
Mordvintsev, Randazzo and Niklasson optimise chemical reaction networks by
differentiable programming,\cite{Mordvintsev2023} Lee, Buse and Winfree extend the
approach to indexed networks and reaction--diffusion systems,\cite{Lee2025} and
Rijal and Mehta differentiate the Gillespie algorithm.\cite{RijalMehta2025} Linder
and Seelig show how activation maximisation can design biological sequences and
discuss the relaxation issues treated here.\cite{LinderSeelig2021}
