ID: DNA-G05
TITLE: Exact filtering and physical obligations
SUBTITLE: A proof about tokens does not certify a molecular experiment
EVIDENCE: Chapter 1 conceptual contract; exact examples are checked separately in code.
LEGEND: DATA → transformation or dependency; CONTROL ⇢ obligation, not a measured causal effect.

NODE n1 | Valid walk population | All walks of lengths 1 to n; adjacency is checked.
NODE n2 | Ideal predicate filters | Endpoints, length n, then coverage of every vertex.
NODE n3 | Surviving witnesses | Every survivor is valid; absence needs completeness.
NODE n4 | Generation obligation | Can the encoding represent and produce each needed candidate?
NODE n5 | Selection obligation | Do physical operations preserve wanted candidates and reject others?
NODE n6 | Readout obligation | Can the measurement detect, decode and independently check a witness?

EDGE n1 → n2 | DATA | apply predicates
EDGE n2 → n3 | DATA | retain witnesses
EDGE n1 → n4 | CONTROL | coverage
EDGE n2 → n5 | CONTROL | selectivity
EDGE n3 → n6 | CONTROL | sensitivity
EDGE n4 → n5 | DATA | track loss
EDGE n5 → n6 | DATA | bound conclusion

READING: The left lane has exact semantics. Each right-lane obligation needs separate experimental evidence.
FAILURE: A missing witness can give an empty readout even when a solution exists; ideal filter correctness is insufficient.
