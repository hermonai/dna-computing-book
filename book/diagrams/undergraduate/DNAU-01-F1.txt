FIGURE DNAU-01-F1: Counting by moving objects
QUESTION: What changes when we add two?
OBJECTS: three numbered counters; two waiting counters; a receiving tray
RELATION: explanatory reading order, not an unlabelled physical cause
STEP 1: Start with three in the tray
STEP 2: Move waiting counter 4
STEP 3: Move waiting counter 5
STEP 1 → STEP 2 → STEP 3
CAPTION: The output is five; moving is the operation, counting is the reading.
LIMIT: Do not create or consume counters between panels.
EVIDENCE: Original arithmetic example.
