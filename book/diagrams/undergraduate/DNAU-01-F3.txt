FIGURE DNAU-01-F3: A quantity is not its carrier
QUESTION: Can three look different and still mean three?
OBJECTS: three counters; numeral 3; three marked cells
RELATION: explanatory reading order, not an unlabelled physical cause
STEP 1: Count objects
STEP 2: Read a numeral
STEP 3: Read a declared one-mark-per-object convention
STEP 1 → STEP 2 → STEP 3
CAPTION: Representations agree only because their reading conventions are stated.
LIMIT: Do not equate information with physical shape or give a formal information measure.
EVIDENCE: Original representation example.
