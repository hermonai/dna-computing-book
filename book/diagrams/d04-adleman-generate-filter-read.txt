RESOURCE LEDGER
  Logical parallelism → molecular population size → material volume and synthesis burden.
  Filter depth        → laboratory stages          → yield loss, error accumulation, and human work.
  Readout resolution  → assay design               → false-positive/false-negative uncertainty.

READING NOTES
1. “Generate” does not enumerate candidates for free; the population is a physical resource.
2. Each filter is both a logical predicate and a noisy laboratory operation with depletion risk.
3. The final claim depends on controls for contamination, assay specificity, and sequence identity.
