D21 — IDEAL, COMPUTATIONAL, AND PHYSICAL DUPLEX REPRESENTATIONS
Scope: shows why a double strand may require more structure than the pair (s, RC(s)).

LEGEND
  ┌─┐ formal object   ╔═╗ physical object   → refinement   ⊣ assumption violation
   ⇢ empirical mapping

┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ IDEAL DUPLEX D₀ = (s⁺, RC(s⁺))                                                     │
│ assumptions: canonical bases · equal length · perfect pairing · no overhang · no nick│
│              no modification · one declared alignment · both sequences oriented     │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                           │ relax one or more assumptions
                                           ▼
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ COMPUTATIONAL DUPLEX D₁                                                            │
│ (strand⁺, strand⁻, alignment, pairing relation, overhangs, nicks, modifications)     │
│ optional weights: mismatch score, uncertainty, provenance, observation quality      │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                           │ physical encoding + environment ⇢
                                           ▼
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║ PHYSICAL DUPLEX D_phys                                                             ║
║ molecular ensemble with conformations, solvent, ions, temperature, concentrations, ║
║ association/dissociation kinetics, competing structures, damage, and measurement    ║
╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

FAILURE PATHS
  mismatch / overhang / nick / modification ⊣ use of D₀ without an explicit approximation;
  D₁ score agreement ⊣ physical stability unless calibrated against observations.

READING NOTES
1. D₀ is an exact and useful object inside its assumptions, especially for sequence algebra.
2. D₁ can represent laboratory constructs more faithfully while remaining a digital model.
3. D_phys is generally an ensemble under conditions, not a single pair of strings.
