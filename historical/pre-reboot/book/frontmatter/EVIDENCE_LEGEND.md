# Evidence legend

Every nontrivial statement in this book belongs to one of the following classes.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Established biology | Reproduced biological knowledge supported by appropriate primary or authoritative literature |
| Established computer science | A standard mathematical or computational result under stated assumptions |
| Established DNA-computing result | A theoretical or experimental result from the DNA-computing literature |
| Source-derived claim | A claim made by an inspected project source, not yet independently established here |
| Our interpretation | A synthesis or proposed reading of sources |
| Engineering analogy | A deliberately limited mapping used to explain or design software |
| Hypothesis | A testable empirical proposition |
| Conjecture | A mathematical statement without an accepted proof here |
| Open question | A question whose answer is not assumed |

The label controls the verb. Sources *report*, specifications *define*, programs *implement*, tests *pass*, experiments *measure*, and proofs *establish*. A later layer never upgrades an earlier claim automatically.
