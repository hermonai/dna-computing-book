READING NOTES
1. The map is useful because it removes variables; that same removal limits the claims it can support.
2. A bare string has no intrinsic molecular concentration, temperature, or conformation.
3. Orientation must be stated outside the letters or fixed by a convention such as 5′→3′ writing.
