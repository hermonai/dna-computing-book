# Complexity and information accounting

For an input of size \(n\), report a resource tuple rather than an unexplained runtime:
\[
(T_{\rm design},T_{\rm reaction},N_{\rm molecules},V_{\rm volume},
 E_{\rm total},T_{\rm read},p_{\rm error}).
\]
This is an accounting checklist, not a claimed new complexity theory.

Generating \(2^n\) assignments in parallel does not make the material requirement polynomial. Increasing copies to improve success probability has costs. Count preparation, amplification, purification and readout as well as reactions. State whether a bound is worst-case, expected or conditional on ideal mixing.

An \(n\)-base canonical string has at most \(2n\) bits of unconstrained symbolic capacity. Physical coding constraints, redundancy and addresses reduce payload. A perfectly complementary second strand is determined by the first under the canonical pairing model; that says nothing about the second strand's chemical function.

Math prerequisites: finite sets, functions, strings, probability, logarithms and asymptotic bounds first; ODEs, Markov processes and thermodynamics when reaction models arrive. No quantum, neural-attention or AGI inference follows from four bases or complementarity.
