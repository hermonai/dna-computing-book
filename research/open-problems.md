# Open problems and next evidence

- Which formal DNA models deserve full chapters versus a comparative section? Resolve through primary constructions and pedagogical examples.
- How faithfully do digital CRN and displacement simulations predict a measured circuit? Require parameter uncertainty and an explicit physical validation set.
- When does spatial localization improve computation after assembly cost is counted?
- Can reusable circuits preserve output separation across cycles? Inspect primary data, not just abstracts.
- Which storage operations are computationally useful under realistic read/write costs?
- What error metric allows fair comparison across circuits, automata and self-assembly?
- Which principles remain useful to computer science without claiming biological equivalence?

No answer is asserted by chapter placement. Each frontier answer needs a source, counterexample and bounded experimental context.
