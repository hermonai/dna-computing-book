# Computational models and proof obligations

A model record must name its state space, transition rule, scheduler, acceptance/output condition and resource measure. Never combine a language-class theorem from one splicing variant with a laboratory implementation of another.

Strand-pool filtering naturally represents nondeterministic candidate generation followed by selection. Sticker occupancy supplies a different state encoding. Splicing and insertion–deletion systems are rewrite formalisms. Watson–Crick automata require an explicit complement relation and head-motion convention. Molecular finite-state machines are not automatically Turing-complete. CRNs admit deterministic concentration and stochastic count semantics; their relation is a modeling limit, not identity. Tile assembly computes through spatial growth under a specified attachment model.

Implementation curriculum: symbolic state machines first; small exhaustive transition checks second; numerical trajectories only after units and rates are defined. D-S03–D-S08 are starting points. Formal variants lacking primary sources remain blocked from theorem-bearing prose.
