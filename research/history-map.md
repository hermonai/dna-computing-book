# Historical map

| Date | Contribution | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1987 | Head introduces splicing formalism (D-S03) | Mathematical model of recombination, before Adleman's experiment. |
| 1994 | Adleman reports a directed Hamiltonian-path instance (D-S01) | One experiment, not a polynomial-time solution for arbitrary instances. |
| 1995 | Lipton proposes DNA approaches to SAT (D-S02) | Proposal and construction must not be narrated as a performed experiment. |
| 2001 | Benenson and colleagues report a programmable molecular machine (D-S04) | Specify finite-state functionality, enzymes and input/output. |
| 2010 | Soloveichik, Seelig and Winfree develop CRN-to-DNA construction (D-S05) | Approximation and kinetic assumptions are part of the result. |
| 2011 | Qian and Winfree demonstrate displacement-circuit composition (D-S06) | Inspect supplements before reproducing counts or yield claims. |
| 2018 | Cherry and Qian study molecular winner-take-all recognition (D-S07) | A molecular classifier is not a general neural training platform. |
| 2019 | Woods and colleagues report reprogrammable algorithmic assembly (D-S08) | Distinguish designed tile set, abstract model and experimental errors. |
| 2023–2024 | Programmable gate arrays and store/compute integration (D-S09, D-S10) | System integration becomes a central question. |
| 2026 | Ternary adder preprint and Evo 2 publication (D-S11, D-S14) | The former is molecular computation; the latter is electronic genomic modeling. |

This is a selected chronology, not a priority-claim registry. Add original self-assembly, origami, storage, gene-circuit and error-correction milestones after source review. Use actual publication/version dates, never search crawl dates.
