# Laboratory reality

No wet-lab protocol is executed in this reset. The book will teach experimental reasoning with published, appropriately scoped examples.

| Operation | Computational abstraction | What the abstraction loses |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hybridization | Compatibility test | Secondary structure, kinetics, concentration and mismatches |
| Ligation | Concatenation | End chemistry, enzyme conditions and competing products |
| Restriction cleavage | Site-directed cut | Duplex recognition, modifications and accessibility |
| PCR | Copy/selection step | Efficiency variation, contamination and amplification bias |
| Gel / affinity selection | Filter | Resolution, recovery yield and false selection |
| Sequencing | Readout | Sampling, errors and instrumentation |
| Synthesis | Input preparation | Yield, sequence constraints, purification and material cost |

For each experiment record concentrations, units, temperature, durations, replicates, controls, losses and readout uncertainty from the original source. Do not fill missing details with plausible numbers. Biomedical relevance is not clinical validation.
