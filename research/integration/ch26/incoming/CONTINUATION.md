# Continuity contract
Canonical DNAD-26 is Laboratory workflows and reproducibility. DNAD-23 separated error
classes; DNAD-24 introduced an auditable evidence contract; DNAD-25 treated storage as
an end-to-end noisy channel. DNAD-26 now organizes a molecular-computation experiment
as a versioned workflow from design freeze through material identity, preparation
records, controls, observation, analysis and evidence release. This is a reproducibility
and data-governance chapter, not a synthesis or hazardous wet-lab protocol.
