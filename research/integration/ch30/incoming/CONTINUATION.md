# Continuity contract
DNAD-29 introduced differentiable molecular/sequence surrogates. DNAD-30 returns to biology before any genomic-computation synthesis. It distinguishes DNA sequence, cis-regulatory elements, trans factors, chromatin/accessibility, RNA, protein, signaling, cell state, development, inheritance and selection. A gene-regulatory-network graph is a model of selected causal relations, not the genome itself. Correlation is not regulation; sequence motif is not occupancy; occupancy is not transcription; RNA abundance is not protein abundance; developmental pseudotime is not literal time. Computational mappings are explicitly labeled hypotheses and handed to Evolutor only after their biological limits are stated.
